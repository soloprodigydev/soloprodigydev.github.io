Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data UNSHADED and this website collect (very little), how it is used, and your rights.

1. The game: no personal data collection

UNSHADED does not collect, store, process or transmit personal information from players. The game contains:

  • No user account system
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No cookies or tracking technologies
  • No server-side data storage

Your save files are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

2. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The home page uses your browser's local storage to remember which Echoes of the Iris you have found, which lore archive entries you have read, whether you have seen certain in-world moments, your sound and volume settings, and choices you make in the small interactive demos on the game page. The soundtrack plays from audio files hosted on this site; nothing is streamed from a third party. That information stays in your browser, is never sent to us, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

3. Platform data (Steam)

When you buy and play UNSHADED through Steam, Valve may collect data as part of its platform services. This may include:

  • Total playtime and session duration
  • Achievement progress (if implemented)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Basic hardware and operating system information

Platform privacy

This information is handled by Valve Corporation under the Steam Privacy Policy. Solo Prodigy Dev does not have access to it and cannot control how Steam processes it.

4. Contacting the developer

If you email Solo Prodigy Dev at soloprodigydev@gmail.com:

  • Your email and message are used only to respond to your inquiry
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for marketing
  • Correspondence may be kept for support purposes only
  • You can ask for your correspondence to be deleted at any time

5. Third-party services

UNSHADED does not integrate with third-party services, including:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics platforms (Google Analytics and similar)
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • External data processors

The only outside service involved in playing the game is the platform you buy it from, such as Steam.

6. Policy changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the game, legal requirements or our practices. Updates will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date, and continued use of UNSHADED after a change means you accept the updated policy.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing on our side to access, change or delete. Your saves remain under your control on your own device.

Questions or concerns? Email soloprodigydev@gmail.com or visit the Contact page.
