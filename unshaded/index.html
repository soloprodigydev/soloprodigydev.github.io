Case file 001

Grain goes missing. Nobody tells the truth.

It starts small, in a quiet valley. Grain disappears from the granary, and a kingdom already split by war has no patience left for small things.

A stranger was seen near the granary. A witness has a grudge. Everyone you meet has something to protect, and a reason to point you somewhere else.

"Echoes show fragments of the past, never answers."

You are not handed a culprit. You gather what people say, what the Echoes show, and what each person stands to lose, then decide for yourself what happened.

    What you actually do

    The whole game is one loop, and Seren pushes back at every step.

    01

    Investigate

    Walk Seren's roads, question its people, and look closely at what they would rather you didn't.

    02

    Listen

    Use the Echoes of the Iris to hear what a place remembers: a glimpse, a sentence, a thing left behind. Never the whole picture.

    03

    Decide

    No narrator names the guilty. Weigh the fragments against what each person stands to lose, and commit to a conclusion.

    04

    Be remembered

    Seren keeps count. People change what they tell you, and what they hide, based on what you have already done.

    Because the Echoes are partial and the people are biased, two players can walk the same road, hear the same fragment, and come away certain of different things.
