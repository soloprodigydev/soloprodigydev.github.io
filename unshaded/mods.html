Modding guidelines

Mods are welcome. Here is what you can make, what you can't, and how to share it.

Allowed

  • Free mods only (no paid content)
  • Texture, audio and UI replacements
  • New content and gameplay tweaks
  • Quality-of-life improvements
  • Safe, respectful, non-harmful content
  • Mods using CC0 assets or original work
  • Content clearly marked as unofficial

Not allowed

  • Paid or monetized mods
  • Hateful, violent or illegal content
  • Story leaks or datamined assets
  • Redistribution of original game code
  • Malicious code or exploits
  • Copyrighted material without permission
  • Mods that harm the game's integrity
  • Commercial derivatives or clones

Sharing your mod

  • Share mods completely free of charge
  • State clearly that your mod is unofficial and fan-made
  • Never reupload the full game or substantial portions of it
  • Include a readme with installation instructions
  • Credit any third-party assets you use (where allowed)
  • Respect the EULA and these guidelines
  • Be respectful to other modders and the community

Use mods at your own risk

Mods are unofficial, fan-made content and are used at your own risk. Solo Prodigy Dev is not responsible for issues, bugs, crashes or data loss caused by third-party modifications. Back up your save files before installing mods.

Not sure if your mod is okay?

Ask before you build it. We are happy to help.
