UNSHADED is a story-driven mystery game from solo developer Solo Prodigy Dev. You investigate a kingdom split by war, walking the roads of Seren, questioning its people and listening to Echoes of the Iris: memories that show fragments of the past but never tell you who is right. There is no narrator to name the guilty, only the evidence you gather and the conclusion you commit to. Seren keeps count, so what you say and do changes how its people treat you.
