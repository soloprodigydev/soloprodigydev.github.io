What is ORISON

One instrument.
Every dimension of sound.

The best tools in music production each own a single domain. Serum owns wavetable. Pigments owns spectral color. Omnisphere owns depth and scale. Switching between them to build a single patch is the norm — and it shouldn't be.

ORISON is built around a simple premise: synthesize without compromise. Wavetable, granular, spectral, and sampler engines running in parallel, governed by a single modulation architecture. Built from scratch. Built to last.

Architecture

Four engines.
One signal path.

01 — Primary
Wavetable
Digital · Surgical · Precise

The backbone. Wavetable synthesis gives ORISON its edge — every harmonic controllable, every morph deliberate. Whether you're carving a bass that cuts or a lead that defines the mix, the wavetable engine handles it with mathematical precision. Inspired by what Serum got right, built without its constraints.
